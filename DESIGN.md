# 宵鐘 YOIGANE (Evening Bell)

Original 64 s animated short. Every picture and every sound is generated by the code in this folder:
no image or audio assets, no TTS, no generative models. Original characters, story, lyrics and music.

## Format
1920x1080, 24 fps, 64 s = 1536 frames. 120 BPM, 4/4, one bar = 2 s = 48 frames, 32 bars.
Every cut lands on a beat (12 frames), so picture and music share one clock.

## Look
- Woodblock base: flat colour, bokashi gradation, visible paper grain, slight block misregistration.
- Line: variable-width ink with tapered ends, heavier on the shadow side and on foreground contours.
- Blue built as two printings: indigo/Prussian key mix under pure Prussian, foam left as bare paper.
- Night snow after shin-hanga: deep navy, steel, ice blue, with small amber windows as the only warmth.
- Compositing on top: bloom, depth haze, light wrap, grain, impact frames.

## Cast (original)
| Name | Role | Silhouette | Clothing | Craft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shio 汐, 16 | ferry keeper's daughter | chin-length blue-black bob, one long side lock bound in vermilion cord with a tiny bell | plain indigo work jacket, seigaiha border only, straw rain skirt | water, bell |
| Hota 焙太, 18 | lantern maker, eats constantly | very broad, dark hair in a stubby topknot, bare arms, crossed tasuki cords, long apron with a lantern mark | none: soot-grey vest and apron; tatewaku appears only as real steam | lantern fire, steam |
| Narumi 鳴海, 20 | festival drummer, few words | tall, cropped black hair, white towel at the neck, short cream happi with one arrow-feather crest | happi; yagasuri appears only as the crest and the feathers | drum, arrow feathers |
| Minato 湊, 7 | Shio's little brother, lost last winter | small, oversized straw coat | plain | none |
| Hikui 灯喰い | eats lamplight and the people near it | three pinched paper lanterns, a printed lamp shade for a face, stolen lamps inside | none | none |

The three carry no swords. Each works with the tool of a trade, its head recast from the broken temple bell: Shio's ferry pole,
Hota's bamboo hatchet, Narumi's drumsticks. The bell tone when one strikes is the film's motif, and each attack grows from its
bearer's trade and textile: seigaiha scales and bell ripples, a lantern built and lit around the target, a drum stroke and a wall of arrow feathers.
